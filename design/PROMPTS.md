# Aurora TV — визуальные ресурсы

Баннер и логотип созданы встроенным инструментом генерации изображений. Финальные PNG включены в Android-проект.

## Баннер

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final Android TV launcher banner, 16:9 landscape. Primary request: refined premium brand banner for a local video player named Aurora TV. Scene/backdrop: deep midnight navy, subtle cinematic aurora ribbon in teal and pale mint flowing horizontally, restrained light and dark contrast. Subject: a simple original triangular play symbol integrated with the aurora ribbon on the left; clean wordmark on the right reading exactly "AURORA TV" in crisp modern sans-serif capitals. Composition/framing: flat front-on graphic, generous safe margins, uncluttered and legible when reduced to 320×180. Color palette: navy #080E17, mint #80E3D4, soft white #EDF6F8. Constraints: exact text AURORA TV once, no other text, no device mockup, no badges, no watermark, no photography. Keep the play symbol simple enough to inspire a vector app icon.

## Логотип

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final square Android TV app icon for Aurora TV, matching the previously generated Aurora TV banner. Primary request: one original minimal play-triangle symbol formed from a softly luminous teal and mint aurora ribbon, with a dark hollow triangle in the center. Style/medium: polished flat-digital brand mark with subtle tonal depth, crisp silhouette and no tiny detail; suitable at 48 px. Composition: centered symbol occupies about 65% of a square canvas with generous safe padding. Color palette: mint #80E3D4 and teal #00CFC0 against transparent background. Constraints: genuinely transparent background, no text, no border, no device mockup, no other symbols, no watermark.
